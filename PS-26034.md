**Title:** Software System to check compliance of Packaged Commodities under Legal
Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 by scanning products, images and labels.

## Background (verbatim gist)
Packaged commodities sold via retail, supermarkets and e-commerce must, under the
Legal Metrology Act 2009 and LMPC Rules 2011, bear mandatory declarations:
manufacturer/packer/importer name and address, net quantity, MRP, month & year of
manufacture/packing/import, consumer care details, and other prescribed declarations
**in a specified format and manner**. Manual inspection by enforcement agencies is
slow and resource-intensive. Common violations: missing declarations, **incorrect font
sizes**, improper MRP declarations.

## What the system must do
- Scan and analyse images of packaged commodities
- Detect the mandatory declarations prescribed under LMPC rules
- Check correctness, **completeness and placement** of declarations
- Identify missing / non-compliant declarations
- Check **readability and font size** requirements
- Generate compliance reports and violation summaries
- Maintain a repository of scanned products + compliance history
- Provide dashboards for enforcement officials

## Expected solution
- Web and/or mobile application
- Automated extraction + validation of mandatory declarations
- **Rule-based** compliance checking against LMPC Rules 2011
- Digital compliance reports in **PDF and editable formats**
- Dashboard for inspections, violations, product compliance
- Search/retrieval of previously scanned products and reports
- Technical documentation: architecture + deployment framework

## Key functional requirements
1. Image upload and product scanning
2. Extraction of declarations from labels/packaging + detection of mandatory ones
3. Font size and readability analysis
4. Detection of missing, misleading or non-standard declarations
5. Compliance / non-compliance report generation
6. Attachment of photographs and supporting evidence
7. Repository of scanned products and inspection history
8. **Role-based access and secure authentication**
9. Dashboard for compliance status and enforcement activities
10. Export of reports to PDF and editable formats

# SIH 2026 process rules (from official Guidelines PDF)

**Deadline: 20 September 2026** — team nomination AND idea submission by SPOC/team
leader. No requests entertained after. (Today: 3 Sep 2026 → ~17 days.)

- Students cannot self-register. College SPOC registers teams after an **internal hackathon**.
- Team = exactly **6 members**, all from the same college, **at least one female member**.
- Max 50 teams per institute (45 + 5 waitlisted); 100 per university.
- Team name must be unique and must **not contain the college name**.
- One team may submit ideas against a **maximum of 2 problem statements**.
- Only **500 ideas per PS**; PS freezes once full. (26034 is at 1/500 — near-zero competition.)
- 4–5 teams per PS shortlisted for the Grand Finale; PS creator isn't obliged to pick a winner.
- Grand Finale: **offline, December 2026**, at nodal centres. Prize ₹1,50,000 per PS.
- IP of the winning idea split equally between the PS-giving organization and the team.
- Idea must be **new** — not shown in any previous event/program.
- Up to 2 optional mentors (5+ yrs experience) after shortlisting.

## What is actually submitted now
The portal submission is: team details (pre-entered), chosen PS, **idea title**, **idea
description**, and an **idea presentation as PDF**. No code, no prototype at this stage —
the working prototype is built at the Grand Finale.

### Idea PPT format (SIH2026-IDEA-Presentation-Format.pptx)
Max **6 slides including the title slide**. Points/diagrams/infographics, not paragraphs.
Template must be used unchanged. Export to **PDF only**.

1. **Title slide** — SIH 2026, PS ID, PS Title, Theme, PS Category (Software), Team ID, Team Name
2. **Proposed Solution** — idea/solution/prototype, how it addresses the problem, innovation & uniqueness
3. **Technical Approach** — technologies (languages, frameworks, hardware), methodology, flowcharts/images/working prototype
4. **Feasibility and Viability** — feasibility analysis, challenges & risks, mitigation strategies
5. **Impact and Benefits** — impact on target audience; social/economic/environmental benefits
6. **Research and References** — links and research details

### Evaluation criteria for idea shortlisting
Novelty, complexity, clarity and detail in the prescribed format, feasibility,
practicability, sustainability, scale of impact, user experience, potential for future work.
